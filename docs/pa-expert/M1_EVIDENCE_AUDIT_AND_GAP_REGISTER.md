## Rules
- Distinguish **PLAN**, **REPOSITORY ARTIFACT**, **OPERATIONAL RECORD VERIFIED**, and **OUTCOME VERIFIED**. A source file/website is not proof of student improvement.
- Public repo holds metadata and redacted summaries only. Real individual data, signed forms, videos, student IDs and teacher evaluations remain private.
- Each claim needs source path, date, responsible reviewer, method, period, sample denominator and limits. No retroactive evidence creation.
- M0-A Source Lock remains pending amendment applicability; M1 can proceed in parallel.
